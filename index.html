Prevention & Safety

Simple steps to reduce mosquito breeding and bites.

Dengue Prevention

  • Remove stagnant water from containers.
  • Cover water storage tanks.
  • Use mosquito repellent.
  • Wear long-sleeved clothing.
  • Use window screens and mosquito nets.

Malaria Prevention

  • Sleep under insecticide-treated nets where recommended.
  • Use mosquito repellent.
  • Fit screens on doors and windows.
  • Remove mosquito breeding sites.
  • Seek medical advice if fever develops.

When to Seek Help

  • Do not ignore fever or unusual symptoms.
  • Consult a qualified healthcare professional.
  • Do not self-medicate.
  • Follow local public-health guidance.
